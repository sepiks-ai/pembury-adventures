# Pembury Adventures

Storybook games for Elin and Alva, from *The Pembury Chronicles*.

- `index.html`: Book One, *The Swords of Pembury*. A single self-contained page; open it in any browser.
- `draw/`: *The Wiggle Machine*. Photograph a drawing; it is cut out of the paper and animated (jelly, dance, explode, melt, blast off, clones and more), with googly eyes, arms and legs (drag dots onto the head, hands and feet; the joints are found where the drawing goes thin), and a video to save. Pictures stay on the device: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Played at https://sepiks-ai.github.io/pembury-adventures/ and https://sepiks-ai.github.io/pembury-adventures/draw/
